**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026

## Brushing duration and caries

Searched for studies relating toothbrushing duration to a caries outcome.
**None identified**, consistent with SIGN 138 §5.5.2, which reached the same
conclusion from its own search.

Searches for duration and **plaque** do return results. That literature exists
and the chapter says so; I have not appraised it, and the chapter's claim about
it is deliberately limited to its existence.

## Bristle stiffness

Full text held for Ranzan N, Muniz FWMG, Rösing CK. Are bristle stiffness and
bristle end-shape related to adverse effects on soft tissues during
toothbrushing? A systematic review. *Int Dent J* 2019.

Its Table 3 rates the quality of evidence **LOW** (⨁⨁◯◯) for both bristle
stiffness and end-shape, downgrading for very serious risk of bias and serious
indirectness, and recording a dose-response gradient under other considerations.
DBOH's own statement is "low certainty evidence of gingival lesions when hard
bristle brushes were used."

An earlier draft of the chapter rated this **very low**, one level below both,
without naming the additional domain. Corrected to low.

## Head size

DBOH provides no component-specific evidence statement or citation for
toothbrush head size, and **I did not search that literature**. The chapter says
the guideline gives no evidence for it, which is a claim about the guideline, not
about the world.

## The two-minute rule

Not a graded recommendation in chapter 2. It **is** in chapter 8, which advises
that thorough cleaning "may take at least 2 minutes," refers to "the recommended
2 minutes," and discusses timers, giving coverage of all surfaces rather than
caries as the rationale. An earlier draft said the rule was not in the guideline
at all.
