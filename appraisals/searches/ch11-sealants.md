## Full text

Held (`documentation/refs/`), including the summary-of-findings tables and their
footnotes. The chapter's statements about the following are from the review body:

- the 24-month estimate covering **children aged 5 to 10**, from 7 trials;
- five of those seven trials published **1976 to 1979**;
- water fluoridation status: 3 fluoridated, 2 not, 2 unreported;
- the GRADE downgrade being for **indirectness** (era), with risk of bias
  explicitly **not** used as a downgrade despite all trials being at high risk of
  detection bias;
- the two modern Chinese trials' control event rates (17% and 15%) against the
  1970s range of 37% to 69%.

The third and fifth of those corrected errors in an earlier draft of the chapter.
