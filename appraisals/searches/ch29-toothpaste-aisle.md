## Scope

This chapter is a consumer guide rather than an audit, and its searches were
correspondingly light. It leans on evidence appraised elsewhere in the book,
principally Walsh 2019 on fluoride concentration (chapter 8), rather than on new
searches of its own.

## What was not searched systematically

Charcoal, whitening, hydroxyapatite, herbal and SLS-free toothpastes. No
systematic search of those literatures was run. Statements about them in the
chapter should be read as informed clinical opinion, not as appraisal, and the
chapter is worded to make that clear.

## High-concentration toothpaste

The chapter's statements about concentrations above 1,500 ppm rest on the
comparisons already tabulated in chapter 8 from Walsh 2019: 1,700 to 2,200 ppm
against 1,450 to 1,500 ppm, SMD 0.04 (95% CI −0.07 to 0.15), indirect evidence
only; and 2,400 to 2,800 ppm against 1,450 to 1,500 ppm, SMD −0.05 (−0.14 to
0.05), two studies. Head-to-head trials **do** exist; what they do not show is
superiority.

DBOH's own adult row for obvious active coronal or root caries adds
moderate-certainty evidence for 5,000 ppm fluoride **for root caries
specifically**, which is a narrower claim than the child 10-and-over row and is
quoted separately in the chapter.
