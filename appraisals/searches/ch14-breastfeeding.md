**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026
**Question:** Is there a systematic review of breastfeeding and dental caries
more recent than Tham 2015?

## Result

None identified that supersedes it for this purpose. Tham R, Bowatte G,
Dharmage SC, and colleagues. Breastfeeding and the risk of dental caries: a
systematic review and meta-analysis. *Acta Paediatr* 2015;104(S467):62-84,
remains the review DBOH relies on for the dental claim. Full text held.

## What DBOH actually footnotes

The chapter 13 clause "low certainty evidence of a dental caries-preventive
effect" carries **two** footnotes, not one: SACN, *Feeding in the first year of
life* (2018), and Tham 2015. The overall-health basis for the Strong label is a
third footnote, the WHO *Global Strategy for Infant and Young Child Feeding*
(2003).

This chapter audits **Tham only**. It does not audit SACN or WHO.

## Limitation

The Strong label rests substantially on infant nutrition, infection and mortality
evidence, which is outside this book's scope and outside my competence. The
chapter says so rather than implying it audited the whole recommendation.
