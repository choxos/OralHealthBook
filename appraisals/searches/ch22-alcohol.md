## What was not obtained

The systematic review of brief alcohol interventions that DBOH cites was **not**
among the supplied documents and was not obtained. The chapter relies on DBOH's
characterization of it and says so.

Worth recording for anyone re-running this: DBOH's own footnote for the Ask row
resolves to Kaner EFS and colleagues' Cochrane review of brief interventions,
whose title names the setting as **primary care populations**. The setting
question the chapter raises is therefore visible from the citation itself, which
is a smaller criticism than the chapter first made.

## What was not audited

The UK Chief Medical Officers' alcohol guidelines, which are the source of the
14-unit figure. Outside this book's scope, stated in the chapter rather than
implied.

## Quotation check

The chapter 2 table 3c wording was checked character by character against
`sources/Books/DBOH_UK/`. An earlier draft had three silent omissions inside
quotation marks in the Act row, one of which ("to keep health risks from alcohol
to a low level, it is safest not to drink more than 14 units a week") converted a
risk-minimising statement into a flat limit. Corrected.
