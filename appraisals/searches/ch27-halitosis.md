## Two sources located after review, and now cited

- **35212093** Memon MA and colleagues. Aetiology and associations of halitosis:
  a systematic review. *Oral Dis* 2023;29(4):1432-1438. doi:10.1111/odi.14172.
  Reports 80% to 90% of halitosis as intra-oral, with coated tongue, periodontal
  disease and poor oral hygiene the principal factors, and 10% to 20% extra-oral
  and associated with systemic disease. This replaced an uncited "85 to 90%
  against 10 to 15%, mostly ENT" split in an earlier draft.

- **31825092** Kumbargere Nagraj S and colleagues. Interventions for managing
  halitosis. *Cochrane Database Syst Rev* 2019;12(12):CD012213.
  doi:10.1002/14651858.CD012213.pub2. 44 randomized trials, 1,809 participants;
  three at low risk of bias, 16 high, 25 unclear; mostly one to four weeks of
  follow-up. Low to very low certainty throughout, and no conclusion possible
  about the superiority of any intervention.

The second is directly relevant to the advice this chapter gives, and an earlier
draft did not mention it. A chapter recommending tongue cleaning while a Cochrane
review of tongue cleaning sits uncited is the failure this book is about.
