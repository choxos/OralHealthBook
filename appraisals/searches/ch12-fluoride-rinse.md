**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026
**Question:** Is there an update to the Cochrane review of fluoride mouthrinses,
and does it address unsupervised home use?

## Query

```
Fluoride mouthrinses for preventing dental caries in children and adolescents[Title]
```

**Result:** no `.pub3`. Marinho VCC, Chong LY, Worthington HV, Walsh T.
*Cochrane Database Syst Rev* 2016;7(7):CD002284.pub2 (PMID 27472005,
doi:10.1002/14651858.CD002284.pub2) remains current. Its own search ran to
**22 April 2016**.

## What the abstract establishes

- 37 trials, 15,813 children and adolescents.
- D(M)FS prevented fraction **27% (95% CI 23% to 30%)**, 35 trials, 15,305
  participants, moderate certainty.
- "All trials tested supervised use of fluoride mouthrinse in schools, **with two
  studies also including home use**."
- "the findings may be applicable to children in other settings with supervised
  or unsupervised rinsing, although the size of the caries-preventive effect is
  less clear."
- 28 of 37 trials at high risk of bias, 9 unclear.

The last three points corrected an earlier draft of the chapter, which argued
that the supervised-to-unsupervised gap went undisclosed.
