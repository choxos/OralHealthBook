**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026
**Question:** Is there an update to the Cochrane review of fluoride varnish, and
is there evidence bearing on the recommended twice-yearly frequency?

## Query

```
Fluoride varnishes for preventing dental caries[Title]
```

Also checked by identifier CD002279.

**Result:** no `.pub3`. Marinho VCC, Worthington HV, Walsh T, Clarkson JE.
Fluoride varnishes for preventing dental caries in children and adolescents.
*Cochrane Database Syst Rev* 2013;(7):CD002279.pub2 remains current.

The review's own search ran to **13 May 2013** across CENTRAL, MEDLINE, EMBASE,
CINAHL, LILACS, BBO, ProQuest Dissertations and Theses, and Web of Science
Conference Proceedings, with no language restriction.

## Full text

Held: full text including characteristics of included studies

adverse-effects gap are from the review body rather than the abstract.

## Not searched

Primary trials of varnish published since 2013. The chapter's "the evidence is
ageing" point rests on the review's own newest included trial being from 2012,
which is a fact about the review, not a claim that nothing has been published
since. That distinction is stated in the chapter.
