# Chapter 19: routine scale and polish

**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026

## Deferring to the Cochrane search

Cited source is a Cochrane review [@lamont2018] whose search ran to **10 January
2018** across the Cochrane Oral Health Trials Register, CENTRAL (2017 Issue 12),
MEDLINE Ovid (1946 onwards) and Embase Ovid (1980 onwards), plus
ClinicalTrials.gov and the WHO ICTRP. No independent search of the primary
literature was attempted.

## Figures extracted, all from the structured abstract

Two trials, 1711 participants, UK general dental practice, adults without severe
periodontitis, regular attenders. Follow-up 24 and 36 months.

Versus no scheduled treatment:

| Outcome | Estimate | certainty |
|---|---|---|
| Gingivitis, 6-monthly | SMD -0.01 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.11), 2 trials, n=1087 | high |
| Gingivitis, 12-monthly | SMD -0.04 (95% CI -0.16 to 0.08), 2 trials, n=1091 | high |
| Calculus, 6-monthly | SMD -0.32 (95% CI -0.44 to -0.20), 2 trials, n=1088 | high |
| Calculus, 12-monthly | SMD -0.19 (95% CI -0.31 to -0.07), 2 trials, n=1088 | high |
| Probing depths, QoL | little or no difference | high |
| Plaque | little or no difference | low |
| Self-reported cleanliness | higher with treatment | low |

Six- versus twelve-monthly: gingivitis SMD 0.03 (95% CI -0.09 to 0.15, I2=0%,
2 trials, n=1090), high certainty; calculus SMD -0.13 (95% CI -0.25 to -0.01),
high certainty.

**Not measured by either trial:** adverse effects, changes in attachment level,
tooth loss, halitosis. No study compared delivery by dentists against therapists
or hygienists. Cost evidence very low certainty.

## Note on interpretation

This is the one chapter where "no demonstrated benefit" means a precise estimate
centred on zero rather than an empty literature. The gingivitis interval
(-0.13 to 0.11) excludes any patient-noticeable effect. Recorded here because the
distinction is easy to lose and the book leans on it elsewhere in the opposite
direction.
