**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 21 August 2026
**Question:** Is there an update to the Cochrane review of water fluoridation?

## Result

The 2024 version is current. Iheozor-Ejiofor Z and colleagues. Water
fluoridation for the prevention of dental caries. *Cochrane Database Syst Rev*
2024;10:CD010856.pub3. Its own search ran to **16 August 2023** across CENTRAL,
MEDLINE, Embase and four other databases, with two trial registers and backward
citation searching.

## Full text

Held, including characteristics of included studies (`documentation/refs/`).

## Key figures used in the chapter, all from the review

- 157 studies, all non-randomised.
- Post-1975 change in dmft: MD 0.24 (95% CI −0.03 to 0.52), 2 studies, 2,908
  children, low certainty.
- Post-1975 change in proportion caries-free, primary dentition: MD −0.04 (−0.09
  to 0.01), 2 studies, 2,908 children; permanent dentition MD −0.03 (−0.07 to
  0.01), 2 studies, 2,348 children. Both low certainty, both favouring
  fluoridation.
- **Sign convention**, from the review's own table footnote: "A negative value
  represents a greater increase in the proportion of caries-free children from
  baseline to follow-up in the water fluoridation group." Negative is benefit.
- Change in DMFT post-1975: 4 studies, 2,856 **children**, very uncertain.
- Cessation: 1 study, could not determine.
- One post-1975 study reported disparities by socioeconomic status.
- No eligible studies reporting caries outcomes in adults.

## Not updated by the 2024 version

Fluorosis. The review did not re-run its fluorosis searches; those figures are
carried forward from the 2015 version. This matters for chapter 25 and is stated
there.
