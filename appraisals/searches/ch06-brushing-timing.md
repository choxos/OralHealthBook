# Chapter 6: timing of toothbrushing and dental caries

**Database:** PubMed
**Date run:** 15 August 2026
**Question:** Does brushing last thing at night, rather than earlier in the
evening, reduce dental caries or tooth pain?

## Query

```
(toothbrushing timing OR time of day brushing OR bedtime brushing) AND dental caries
```

PubMed's translation expanded this across `toothbrushing[MeSH]`, `time[MeSH]`,
"bedtime", and `dental caries[MeSH]`.

**249 records.** Top 20 by relevance reviewed by title and abstract.

## Result: nothing directly relevant

Not one record compared brushing at one time of day against brushing at another
with a caries or patient-important outcome.

The top hit was 27334438 (Kumar 2016), which is about **frequency**, not timing,
and is handled in chapter 5. The remainder were about brushing frequency,
supervised brushing programmes, or fluoride kinetics.

## What the recommendation actually rests on

Traced by hand rather than by search, through the citation chain:

1. *Delivering Better Oral Health* 2025, chapter 13, tables 1 and 6 → cites SIGN 138.
2. SIGN 138 §5.7.2 → cites its reference 101, and attaches **no evidence grade**.
3. SIGN 138 reference 101 = Duckworth RM, Moore MS. Salivary fluoride
   concentrations after overnight use of toothpastes. *Caries Res*
   2001;35(4):285.

Reference 101 verified against the SIGN 138 PDF (`sources/Books/SIGN_Scot/`),
page 50. It is a one-page abstract in the 48th ORCA congress supplement. It has
no DOI and no PubMed record, which is why it cannot be found by any search and
had to be traced through the printed reference list.

## Conclusion

These searches identified no trial comparing toothbrushing at different times of
day with a caries outcome. Given that only the top 20 of 249 records were
screened, in one database, by one person, that is a statement about the search
and not about the literature. The chapter is worded accordingly: absence of
evidence found, not evidence of absence.

## Limitation

PubMed only, one screener, titles and abstracts of the top 20 of 249 by
relevance rather than all 249. A trial buried below rank 20 with an
uninformative title could have been missed.
