## Consequences, stated in the chapter

- **Oil pulling.** No search was run. The chapter therefore does not characterise
  the size, duration or comparators of that trial literature, and confines itself
  to saying that it would not trade brushing time for it on mechanism alone, and
  that aspiration lipoid pneumonia is reported in case reports.
- **Water flossers.** Statements rest on Worthington 2019, already appraised in
  chapter 17. Against toothbrushing alone, oral irrigators may reduce the gingival
  index at one month (SMD −0.48, 95% CI −0.89 to −0.06, 4 trials, 380
  participants, very low certainty) but not at three or six months, and did not
  reduce bleeding sites or plaque. Against floss, short-term gingivitis may favour
  irrigators. There is **no** irrigator-versus-interdental-brush comparison in the
  review.
- **Wisdom teeth.** NICE TA1 concerns **pathology-free impacted** third molars.
- **Amalgam.** The UK restriction covers deciduous teeth, patients under 15, and
  pregnant or breastfeeding women, except where the practitioner judges it
  strictly necessary.
