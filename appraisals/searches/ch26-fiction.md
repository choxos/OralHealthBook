## Figures used

All intervals from FiCTION are **97.5% confidence intervals**, reflecting the
trial's three-arm structure, and the chapter labels them as such throughout.

- Conventional treatment versus biological management: adjusted risk difference
  −2% (97.5% CI −10% to 6%).
- Conventional versus prevention alone: 4% (97.5% CI −4% to 12%).
- Episodes, adjusted incident rate ratios: 0.95 (97.5% CI 0.75 to 1.21) and 1.18
  (0.94 to 1.48).

## Powering

The original power calculation was for the **incidence** co-primary only. The
comparison of mean number of episodes was explicitly unpowered and reported as an
exploratory hypothesis test. The chapter states this.

## Retention

1,144 randomized; 86 (8%) attended no study visits; 1,058 formed the modified
intention-to-treat set. So 92.5% contributed **at least one** analysed visit,
which is not the same as retention to 33.8 months. A sensitivity analysis
restricted to participants with at least 23 months of follow-up.

## Conclusion wording

Quoted in full, including the qualifier an earlier draft dropped: "there was no
evidence of a difference among the 3 treatment approaches for incidence or number
of episodes of dental pain **and/or** infection experienced by these participants
**with high caries risk and established disease**."
