\noindent \textbf{A note on what this book is not.} This book examines the
research evidence behind public oral health recommendations. It is not medical
or dental advice, it is not a substitute for examination by a qualified
clinician, and nothing in it should be used to diagnose or treat a condition.
Where the book concludes that the evidence for a piece of advice is weaker than
its official label suggests, that is a statement about the research, not an
instruction to stop following the advice. Chapter~31 sets out what the author
does and why.\par
